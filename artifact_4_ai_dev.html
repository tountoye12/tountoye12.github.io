Artifact: Explainable AI & Model Trust

This artifact explores the importance of Explainable AI (XAI) in modern machine learning systems. It highlights challenges in model transparency, validation techniques, and performance metrics used to ensure reliability and trust.

Explainable AI Infographic

Overview

Explainable AI (XAI) focuses on making AI systems transparent and understandable. Models such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, and LLaMA are powerful but often operate as black boxes, making it difficult to interpret how decisions are made. This creates challenges in trust, accountability, and ethical AI use.

Key Challenges

  • High model complexity with billions of parameters
  • Opaque decision-making processes
  • Trade-off between accuracy and interpretability
  • Regulatory requirements for transparency

Validation & Metrics

AI systems are evaluated using performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and fairness indicators. Validation techniques like cross-validation, human evaluation, and adversarial testing ensure robustness and reliability.

Conclusion

Explainability, validation, and performance metrics work together to build trust in AI systems. As AI continues to evolve, these elements are essential for responsible and effective deployment.
